# THE LIST's City and Its Machines — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Deliver `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-list-city-design.md`: an animated 8-bit city behind THE LIST and,
in its margins, the trade's tools as exploded 3D pixel machines floating in the site's art direction. The reading
column stays plain black under every title.

**Architecture:**
- `works/pixel-city.ts` plans the city and its clock (pure).
- `works/machines.ts` holds the machines' view, light, inks, clock and screens (pure).
- `works/list-art.ts` lays out the margins (pure).
- `works/list-machines.ts` builds and draws the machines (three.js, loaded lazily).
- `works/list-deco.ts` paints the city, mounts the machines and the hardware, and runs the 12-step clock.
- `pages/works.ts` starts and stops it with the list.
- `components.css` holds the black band and the hardware.

**Tech Stack:** canvas 2D, three.js (orthographic, ShaderMaterial, a render target with a depth texture), CSS, vitest;
Playwright with Chrome (SwiftShader) for the renders.

## Global Constraints

- `about-old.*` is never touched. Nothing is ever drawn under a title: the band is CSS.
- Motion goes through `reducedMotion()` only. Phones are gated on coarse pointers, and nothing overflows at 375 or 390.
- Gates before commit: `npm run check`, `npm test`, `npm run build`.
